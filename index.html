How to build

  • Look around: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag (or two fingers) to move.
  • Place a block: pick one below, then click the ground or another block.
  • Remove a block: right-click it, or hold Shift and click. (The Erase tool does the same.)
  • Build fast: Box fills a whole area — click one corner, then the opposite corner. Hold Shift on the second click to clear a box.
  • Copy a block: Pick takes the block you click on.
  • Copy and paste: Select — click one corner, then the opposite corner; it is copied automatically. Then Paste: a green footprint follows your pointer — move it where you want, click to place it, Rotate (or Q) to turn it. Click Done or press Esc when finished. With an area selected, press Delete to remove everything in it.
  • Name things: Sign puts floating text in your scene.
  • Mistake? Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes it.
  • Share it: Picture saves a PNG you can attach to a post. Share link copies a link so classmates can walk around your scene.

Keys: 1–9 pick blocks · B build · E erase · X box · S select · Delete clears the selection · C copy · P paste · I pick · T sign · R spin. Your diorama saves itself in this browser.
